RoamRadar — Privacy Policy

RoamRadar is a personal, self-hosted travel planner, run by its owner for their own use. All data is stored in the owner's own Cloudflare account.

Google access: RoamRadar connects to the owner's own Google account. Google Calendar is used to read the owner's events to build trip timelines, and to write one event per trip back to the owner's calendar. Gmail is read only to find the owner's own travel booking confirmations.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking is collected by this app.

The owner can disconnect Google access at any time in the app's Settings, or revoke it from their Google account permissions page.
